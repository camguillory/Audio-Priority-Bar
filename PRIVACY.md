# Privacy

Audio Priority Bar collects no data. There is no account, analytics, or
telemetry, and your settings stay in the app's local preferences.

The only network requests are for updates: once a day the app asks GitHub
for the latest release, and when there is a newer one it downloads and
installs it. Nothing about you or your Mac is sent. You can turn automatic
updates off in Settings.

Input Monitoring, if you grant it, is used only to read whether a Jabra Link
dongle's headset is on. Accessibility, if you allow it in Settings, is used
only to check whether the pointer is on the menu bar icon before showing its
preview. The **Microphone muted** reminder checks whether a
microphone is in use, and never records or reads audio.

**Keep a debug log** is off unless you turn it on in Settings. It writes your
device names, what you click in the app and what the app did to
`~/Library/Logs/Audio Priority Bar` on your Mac, one file per day, and keeps
the last 7 days. **Clear** in Settings deletes them. The app never sends the
log anywhere; attach it to an issue only if you choose to.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/camguillory/Audio-Priority-Bar/issues).
